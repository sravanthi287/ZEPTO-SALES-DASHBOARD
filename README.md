## ZEPTO SALES DASHBOARD(Interactive Sales Dashboard created using Microsoft Excel)

## PROJECT OBJECTIVES
-Analyze Zepto sales and business performance.
-Monitor key business KPIs such as sales, orders, and quantity.
-Compare sales across different cities and customer segments.
-Identify top-performing products and categories.
-Analyze customer types and payment methods.
-Understand monthly sales trends.
-Support business decision-making through interactive visualizations.


## Dataset used

-<a href="https://github.com/sravanthi287/ZEPTO-SALES-DASHBOARD/blob/main/ZEPTO%20SALES%20DASHBOARD.xlsx">Dataset</a>

## Questions (KPI)
-What is the total sales generated?
-How many orders were placed?
-How many customers and products are included?
-What is the total quantity sold?
-What is the average sales value?
-Which city generates the highest sales?
-How do sales compare across customer segments?
-Which customer type contributes the most sales?
-How do sales vary across months?
-Which payment methods are commonly used?
-Which orders have the highest sales?
-How does sales performance vary by category and customer type?

## Dashboard
-The Zepto Sales Dashboard provides an interactive view of business performance using Excel charts, KPI cards, and filters.

## Project Insights
-Total Sales: 563,992.30
-Total Orders: 1,500
-Total Customers: 300
-Total Products: 200
-Total Quantity: 4,207
-Average Sales: 375.99
-Bangalore recorded the highest total sales among the three cities.
-The Office customer segment generated the highest sales.
-Bulk customers contributed the largest share of sales.
-January recorded the highest sales among the available months.
-UPI was the leading payment method by total sales in the dataset.
-The dashboard helps analyze sales trends, customer segments, and payment patterns.
